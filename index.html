LähiTapiola · Financial services & insurance

One coherent experience across a complex organisation.

As outsourced design lead I led a team of about ten designers developing LähiTapiola’s digital service structure, a new visual language for digital channels and the Duet design system.

Role
Design Lead
Period
04/2022 – 09/2026
Team
~10 designers, Duet developer team
Scope
Design leadership, design system, concept & service design, UX/UI, prototyping

01 · Leadership

Consistent quality, and a team that feels safe.

My job was to keep design quality consistent across teams, mentor and support the designers and make knowledge sharing a habit. I lead with a humble approach that values each person’s strengths. For creativity to flourish, everyone has to feel secure and know their ideas are valued.

02 · Exploration

Finding better ways to build the products.

Alongside delivery, I explored new directions and supported initiatives from elsewhere in the organisation: understanding user needs, finding pain points and testing ideas such as a progressive web app and a messaging hub that brings customer communication into one place.

03 · Duet design system

Two brands, one system, one click.

I led the development of Duet, which serves both the LähiTapiola and Turva brands, together with a small developer team. Duet’s components and definitions live in Figma too. With design tokens and variables, a layout switches between the two brands at the click of a button.

Hintsa Performance · Performance coaching

A premium coaching programme, made scalable.

Hintsa coaches elite athletes and top business leaders. As Head of Brand and Design I created a remote coaching service around monthly coach meetings, the Better Life app and the Hintsa Coach tool.

Role
Head of Brand & Design, Lead Designer
Period
04/2016 – 12/2021
Setting
In-house
Scope
Concept & service design, branding, UX/UI, visual design, design system

01 · The journey

Six months, four sprints, one personal coach.

The programme was structured into four six-week sprints, each on one element of wellbeing: nutrition, mental energy, recovery and biomechanics. The Better Life app delivered weekly audio lessons and daily tips from Hintsa specialists, and acted as the programme journal.

02 · Coach tool

Everything the coach needs, in one view.

Activity targets for endurance, strength and mobility were set together with the coach, and clients could always reach their coach in the app. The app also forwarded wellbeing data from clients’ wearables to the Hintsa Coach tool, which made monitoring progress and giving feedback easy, with a content library to support coaching.

Totaljobs, Stepstone Group · Recruitment (UK)

Making job search fun and intuitive.

Totaljobs, one of the UK’s largest hiring platforms, asked us to challenge them with new ways to discover job opportunities on mobile.

Role
Product Designer
Period
02/2022 – 03/2022
Deliverable
Detailed Figma prototype
Scope
Concept & service design, conversation design, UX/UI, prototyping, motion

01 · Concept

A conversation instead of a search form.

With more than 280,000 live job ads, the challenge was helping people narrow things down without feeling like they were filling in a form. We explored several concepts and developed one further: a conversational timeline with suggestions, simple tools for narrowing down listings and scrollable job cards.

02 · Job cards

Just the right information per card.

More focused job cards made browsing more enjoyable, with quick ways to filter results and save listings for later. The final concept was built into a detailed Figma prototype that became the starting point for Totaljobs’ internal product team.

Prego · Hospitality

A name, an identity and sharper flows.

Prego is a booking system built by restaurateurs to help restaurants give better service. The team, then called Core Dining, asked for a distinct identity and help with their core user flows.

Role
Designer
Period
03/2022 – 04/2022
Handover
UI kit for the in-house team
Scope
Naming, branding, visual design, UX/UI, motion design

01 · Identity

Built on caring and passionate.

I created the new name and identity around the core values caring and passionate. The name reflects the service attitude, and the simple brand mark is based on the floor-plan icon of a “2-top”: a table with two chairs.

02 · Booking flow

Booking that looks at home anywhere.

The core booking flows were tuned for ease of use. A key part of the service is embedding the booking tool in each restaurant’s own digital channels, so it adapts to their brand without looking out of place.

Approach

Strategy, systems and a steady hand.

Five things I bring to every team, whether I’m leading designers or doing the design myself.

  1. 01

    Brand and product are one conversation.

    Fifteen years in advertising taught me how brands earn attention. A decade in product design taught me how services earn trust. I bring both to the table, so the brand shows up in how a service works, not just how it looks.

  2. 02

    Design systems that people and AI can build with.

    A well-structured system is the foundation for AI-assisted design and development. In Duet, components, tokens and variables are organised so both designers and AI tools can read them and produce consistent, on-brand results in Figma and in code. Systems keep quality consistent and free teams to focus on the problems that matter.

  3. 03

    Prototype early, prototype real.

    A working prototype settles more debates than a slide deck. The Totaljobs concept became a detailed Figma prototype for the product team; the News Corp pilot ended in a working iOS prototype within a month.

  4. 04

    Lead by making people feel safe.

    Good ideas come from people who feel secure and respected. I mentor, share knowledge and build a collaborative, fun environment, and I keep a humble approach that values each person’s strengths.

  5. 05

    Curious about what comes next.

    I’m passionate about AI in design and how it changes both the work and the services we build, and about mentoring the next generation of designers.

About

Two careers, one craft.

I started designing digital services in Finland in the late 1990s, spent fifteen years in advertising and have focused on digital product design since 2014.

Story

My career bridges two worlds. At Grey and TBWA\Helsinki I worked on top-tier brand and campaign work, ending as partner and lead creative. Since 2014 I’ve focused on digital product design: UX, service design and complex systems, at Avaus, Siili, Reaktor and DK&A, now part of NoA Ignite.

As Head of Brand and Design at Hintsa Performance I built digital coaching tools from the ground up while shaping the brand. At LähiTapiola I led a team of designers and the Duet design system. Along the way I’ve worked with clients such as HBO, News Corp, MTV3, Rovio, KONE and Totaljobs.

I hold a degree in graphic and advertising design and studied technology early on, which is probably why I enjoy both the analytical and the aesthetic side of the work. Outside work you’ll most likely find me climbing.
